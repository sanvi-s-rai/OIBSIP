01 — ABOUT

The Man Behind
the Vision

Vikram Ambalal Sarabhai was born on 12 August 1919 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. He came from a well-known industrial family but chose science as his path. His curiosity and interest in scientific research shaped his remarkable career.

After studying at Cambridge, Sarabhai returned to India and founded the Physical Research Laboratory in Ahmedabad in 1947. The institution became an important centre for research in physics and space science.

Sarabhai understood the importance of space technology for a developing country. He believed that satellites could help India in areas such as communication, education, weather forecasting and national development.

His vision eventually led to the development of India's space programme. His leadership played a major role in the establishment of ISRO and laid the foundation for India's future achievements in space exploration.

04 — LEGACY

His Vision Still
Lives On

Vikram Sarabhai's greatest contribution was not only his scientific work, but also his ability to imagine what science could do for India.

His vision helped create a generation of scientists and institutions that continued to develop India's capabilities in space science and technology.

Today, India's achievements in space stand as a reflection of the foundations laid by visionaries like Sarabhai.
